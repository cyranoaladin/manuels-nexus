\begin{corrige}{1SPE-TRIGO-EX-023}
$\cos^2(x) = 1 - \sin^2(x) = 1 - \dfrac{9}{25} = \dfrac{16}{25}$. Comme $x \in \left[0 \,;\, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$, $\cos(x) \geq 0$, donc $\cos(x) = \dfrac{4}{5}$.
\end{corrige}
