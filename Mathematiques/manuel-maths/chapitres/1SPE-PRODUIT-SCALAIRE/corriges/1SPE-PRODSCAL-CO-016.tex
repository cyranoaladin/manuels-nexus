\begin{corrige}{1SPE-PRODSCAL-EX-016}

$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \dfrac{\|\vec{u} + \vec{v}\|^2 - \|\vec{u}\|^2 - \|\vec{v}\|^2}{2} = \dfrac{49 - 9 - 25}{2} = \dfrac{15}{2}$.
