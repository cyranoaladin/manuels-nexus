\begin{corrige}{TSPE-COMBI-EX-006}
D'apres la relation de Pascal, $\dbinom{7}{3} = \dbinom{6}{2}+\dbinom{6}{3}$.
\[
  \binom{7}{3} = 15+20 = \boxed{35}.
\]
\end{corrige}
