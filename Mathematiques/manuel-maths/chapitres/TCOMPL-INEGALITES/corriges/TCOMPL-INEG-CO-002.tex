\begin{corrige}{TCOMPL-INEG-EX-002}
On calcule d'abord $\displaystyle\int_0^1 L(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = \int_0^1 \frac{2x^2+x}{3}\,\mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{3}\left[\frac{2x^3}{3}+\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{3}\times\frac{7}{6} = \frac{7}{18}$.

D'où :
\[
  G = 1 - 2\times\frac{7}{18} = 1 - \frac{7}{9} = \frac{2}{9} \approx 0{,}22.
\]
\end{corrige}
