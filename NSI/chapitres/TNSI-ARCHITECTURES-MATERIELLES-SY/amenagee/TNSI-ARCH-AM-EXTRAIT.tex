\subsection*{Exercice 2 --- Un interblocage (C4)}

Trois processus, trois ressources :

\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{P1} retient \texttt{R1} et attend \texttt{R2} ;
  \item \texttt{P2} retient \texttt{R2} et attend \texttt{R3} ;
  \item \texttt{P3} retient \texttt{R3} et attend \texttt{R1}.
\end{itemize}

\bigskip

\textbf{Étape 1.} Complète les deux tableaux. La première ligne de chacun est faite.

\bigskip

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Processus} & \textbf{Attend} \\
\hline
\texttt{P1} & \texttt{R2} \\
\hline
\texttt{P2} & \dotfill \\
\hline
\texttt{P3} & \dotfill \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\qquad
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Ressource} & \textbf{Retenue par} \\
\hline
\texttt{R1} & \texttt{P1} \\
\hline
\texttt{R2} & \dotfill \\
\hline
\texttt{R3} & \dotfill \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\bigskip
